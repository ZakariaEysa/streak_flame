## 0.1.0

Initial release.

- Requires Flutter 3.38.1+ and Dart 3.10+.

- Animated red/orange streak flame with a day-count badge, inline celebration
  card, and modal celebration helper.
- Custom palettes, gradients, shapes, borders, typography, and independent flame,
  badge, header, card, and action styling.
- Automatic count transitions, replay/pause/resume controller, configurable
  timing, idle flicker, glow, particles, and optional haptics.
- Localizable labels, number and plural formatting, inherited RTL, and separate
  messages for streak increases and restarts.
- Responsive sizing, scrollable celebration content, reduced-motion support,
  stable screen-reader labels, and app lifecycle/TickerMode handling.
- Standalone example, interactive playground, customization documentation, and
  regression tests.
